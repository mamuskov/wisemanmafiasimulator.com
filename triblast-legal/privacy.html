Privacy Policy: Triangle Block Puzzle: Hexa (TriBlast)

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Mahmut Akyol ("we") makes the game Triangle Block Puzzle: Hexa. This page explains what the game does with information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score, how many games you have finished (used to keep the first games ad-free) and whether you bought "Remove ads". These are stored on your device only. There is no account, and we do not ask for your name, email or location.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device and usage information to deliver ads: advertising identifiers, device and app information, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

  • Personalised ads happen only if you allow it. On iPhone the game asks for Apple's tracking permission. In the EU, UK and Switzerland Google's consent form is shown first. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and, where the form applies, through the "Privacy" link in the game.
  • Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check that the purchase is valid and to restore it. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not run our own analytics servers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The game is not directed at children.

Your choices and rights

You can delete all game data by deleting the app. If you are in a region with data-protection rights (for example the EU/UK), you can contact us to ask about, correct or delete data we hold, which is very little because nothing is collected on our own servers.

Changes

If the game starts using analytics or other services, we will update this page first.
